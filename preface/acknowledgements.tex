\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
As this university journey comes to an end, I wish to express my most sincere gratitude to all the people who stood by my side and whom I had the privilege of knowing over the course of these two years. \\
\\
First and foremost, I would like to thank Dr. Marco Fiorucci, supervisor of this thesis, for his invaluable guidance, constant support, and patience. His advice was essential not only for the development of this work, but also for my academic and personal growth. \\
\\
A special thank you goes to my classmates, who made these two years unforgettable. Between endless project hours, laughter, and shared moments, this journey would not have been the same without your companionship. \\
\\
A heartfelt thank you goes to my lifelong friends, who have stood by my side through everything life has thrown our way—the best moments and the toughest times alike. After more than a decade together, our bond remains as strong as ever. Thank you for always being there.\\
\\
An affectionate, deeply fond thought goes to my uncles, aunts, cousins, and my entire extended family, whose smiles and warmth have been a constant refuge on my hardest days. A special, heartfelt place is reserved for my "zio bello", with whom each coffee is so much more than a routine—it is a cherished moment of connection, laughter, and a bond that means the absolute world to me. And a big, warm hug goes to my other uncle, with whom I share our burning, unconditional passion for AC Milan: thank you for every match lived together, every cheer, and for turning pure sport into unforgettable family memories. Having you in my corner makes me feel immensely blessed. \\
\\
An immense, boundless thank you to my family: to my father Bashkim, my mother Saimira, and my sister Sara. You are the roots that hold me steady and the wings that allow me to fly; the fundamental pillar that supported my every doubt and the silent strength pushing me to never settle. Together, we turned every small victory into pure celebration, and in your arms I always found my safest harbor when the world felt overwhelming.

\noindent Mum and Dad, there are no words profound enough to capture what you have done for us. You took the greatest gamble of your lives: you chose courage over comfort, leaving behind your homeland, your history, and everything you knew to cross into the unknown with nothing but hope in your hearts and the sole purpose of gifting us a better future. Every sacrifice you made, every silent hardship you endured without ever letting us feel the weight of it, is carved into the man I have become. This degree is not just my achievement; it is the living proof of your bravery, the fruition of your tears, and the victory of your selfless love. I will spend my entire life striving to make you proud and repaying even a fraction of what you have gifted me.

\noindent To Sara, my sister, my confidante, and the person everyone would wish to have in their corner: thank you for walking this road with me. You have been a constant light, cheering for me in every choice, understanding my unspoken thoughts, and reminding me of who I am whenever I lost my way. Having you as a sister is one of the greatest blessings of my life.

\noindent This milestone, every page of this work, and every step I will take tomorrow belong entirely to you. You are my greatest pride, yesterday, today, and forever.\\
\\
A special place in my heart is reserved for my grandparents. I carry with me the sweet memory of grandmother Lule, with whom I had the joy of celebrating my Bachelor’s degree, and who has recently left us. A thought full of love goes to all of them, watching over me from above: I am certain they are celebrating this day together with me. Your love accompanies me beyond any border, protecting and guiding me every single day. \\
\\
Last, but certainly not least, my whole heart goes to Sandy. You were an unexpected miracle, arriving softly when I least expected anyone and becoming everything I needed. Thank you for holding my hand through every fear, for being my calm amidst the noise, and for making me feel truly safe and understood. With you it truly felt like \textit{«stare sul tetto dell'universo»}, because you \textit{«mi hai tirato su quando sembravo un puzzle»} and \textit{«tutto questo lo fai diventare bellissimo»}. Walking beside you makes every dream feel possible, and this victory belongs to both of us.
